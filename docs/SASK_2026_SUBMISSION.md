# Startup Awards Slovensko 2026 — what the application needs

**Researched 2026-09-28.** Sources are linked per fact; where the organisers publish nothing, this
document says so rather than guessing.

> **The deadline is 30 September 2026.** Two days from the date of this research. The SASK 2026
> overview page still shows the original **14 September**, but the application page and the press
> coverage of the extension both say **30 September**, at
> [`sask.sk/prihlaska-startup-awards/`](https://sask.sk/prihlaska-startup-awards/).

---

## 1. What the application consists of

| | |
|---|---|
| **form** | Tally, [`tally.so/r/5BgD9v`](https://tally.so/r/5BgD9v) for standard startups. Next Gen (student) teams use a separate Google Form, [`forms.gle/6FaKkMML2d7q1DaL7`](https://forms.gle/6FaKkMML2d7q1DaL7). |
| **form content** | problem, solution, market, traction, team, fundraising |
| **attachments** | *“pitch deck a krátke video o projekte v anglickom jazyku”* — a pitch deck **and a short video about the project, in English** |
| **eligibility** | early-stage startups with at least one Slovak founder, or a legal entity in Slovakia |
| **categories** | **Lifestyle** (how people live, communicate, spend time) · **Enterprise** · **Deeptech** · **Next Gen** (students) |

**IronPal fits Lifestyle.** Deeptech is arguable given the on-device recognition model, but Lifestyle
is the honest read of a consumer fitness wearable, and category mismatch is an unforced error.

## 2. What the organisers do NOT specify about the video

Checked across the SASK 2026 page, the application page and the press coverage. **None of the
following is published anywhere:**

- length in minutes or seconds — only the word *krátke*, “short”
- file format or codec
- aspect ratio
- resolution
- whether it must show the founder, the product, or a demo
- whether it is strictly required or merely recommended

The application page words it as preparatory material rather than a hard gate, so treat the video as
**strongly expected but not spec'd**. The one requirement that *is* explicit is the language: it must
be **in English**.

## 3. Where this collides with the promo being built

The film in [`IRONPAL_PROMO_VIDEO_SCRIPT_V1.md`](IRONPAL_PROMO_VIDEO_SCRIPT_V1.md) is not this video,
and the difference is not cosmetic.

| | the promo | what SASK asks for |
|---|---|---|
| audience | a lifter scrolling social | a judging panel |
| job | make someone want the product | explain the project |
| content | problem, product, privacy, progress, call to action | problem, solution, market, traction, team, fundraising |
| shape | vertical 9:16, eight 8-second clips | unstated; a panel watches on a laptop, so 16:9 is the safer default |
| founder | plays a character delivering a pitch | is the founder, on the record |

**The promo is a consumer advertisement. The submission wants a project explainer.** They overlap on
problem and solution and diverge everywhere else — there is nothing in the promo about market size
as an opportunity, nothing about traction, nothing about the team, nothing about the raise.

**It also will not be finished in time.** Two of eight clips exist. The remaining six need
generation, retries against policy refusals, and five pieces of product footage that have not been
recorded (the three POC screen recordings, the manual-log mock, the privacy graphic). That is not
two days of work.

## 4. Recommendation

**Do not try to finish the promo for this deadline.** Submit something built for the panel, and keep
the promo on its own schedule for the campaign it was designed for.

What a SASK video needs, and what already exists to make it from:

1. **The founder to camera, in English, stating the problem and the product.** K1 and K2 are already
   rendered and are exactly this register — the industry figures and the manual-logging complaint.
   They are 9:16, which is the one thing to weigh.
2. **The product.** `web/public/assets/reveal.mp4` for the physical band, plus the studio and worn
   stills the landing page uses.
3. **Proof it works.** The POC on the A52: the live HUD during a set, the post-set debrief, the level
   progress. **These do not exist yet and are the highest-value thing to record**, because traction
   and a working product are what a judging panel is actually assessing.
4. **The team.** One founder, stated plainly.

**The honest minimum**, achievable in two days: record the POC screens on the phone, cut them against
K1 and K2, and end on a plain statement of where the product is and what the raise is for. That is a
truer submission than a polished advertisement, and panels reward the former.

## 5. Open questions worth an email

The organisers publish no video spec, and two answers would change the edit. Worth asking
[SAPIE](https://sapie.sk/news/sask), who run it:

- **Maximum length?** “Short” is doing a lot of work. Most such competitions land between 60 and 180
  seconds.
- **Aspect ratio?** Everything rendered so far is 9:16 vertical. If the panel views on a laptop, a
  16:9 cut is worth building instead, and that decision should be made before more clips are
  generated.
