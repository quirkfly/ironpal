## Description

```
Billions have gone into AI, and the smartest thing in your gym is still your thumb.

I got tired of typing my sets into an app, so I started building IronPal: a headband
with a tiny camera and motion sensors that watches your set and fills in the log
itself. No buttons. No pausing between sets.

How it's being built: a 200-degree fisheye camera module and an Arduino motion-sensor
board, wired to the phone, and an app that counts reps from motion and learns your
exercises as you tag them.

What it does:
- Recognises the exercise and counts the reps from motion, on your phone
- Reads the working weight from a single frame - sent for processing, then deleted
- Checks your form as you go: depth, back angle, knee travel

Reserve your spot at https://ironpal.co - it costs nothing today, and it tells me
how many of these to build.

---
Pre-launch. The app screens in this video are a product interface concept, not a
shipped release, and weight reading is still in validation. I'm a solo founder
building this in the open.

This video was made with AI: the presenter and the lab and gym scenes are generated,
including the soldering and coding scenes, which depict the build rather than
record it.

Music: "Dreaming Big" via Mixkit (Mixkit Stock Music Free License).

#fitnesstech #wearable #strengthtraining
```

build. The new line says exactly what is generated, and that the build scenes depict rather than
